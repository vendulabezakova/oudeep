Zobraziť celé →

Learning & Development was an experimental project at our company, designed to hone the skills and abilities of our employees. My team was among the first to participate in this initiative, and we were pleasantly surprised by the many interesting insights and experiences it continues to provide to this day.

Calling it a success would be an understatement. The L&D team managed to create an entire development plan from the ground up in just a few months, implementing advanced methods and tools such as Gallup Strengthsfinder, skill gap analysis, SHL Competency Profiler cards, and many more.

For me, the Gallup CliftonStrengths Assessment was probably the most interesting aspect. Each member of my team completed the Gallup test to discover their individual strengths, and we didn't stop there. We learned a great deal about our personalities, how our strengths manifest in our daily lives, and how they influence our colleagues and performance. Simply put, we realized what we're good at and how to grow.

The entire team participated in multiple sessions where we learned how to prioritize, communicate assertively, and promote responsibility and accountability. The feedback from everyone who participated was overwhelmingly positive.

The L&D team focused on both our soft and hard skills, organizing numerous meetings with experts across the company. The result was a significant increase in our knowledge of the company and industry context. L&D taught us more about our colleagues and their roles than any previous huddle or meeting.

It has been two years since the L&D project concluded, and my only regret is that we couldn't continue due to shifting company priorities. Some of us were initially skeptical about the viability of such a project. However, Nikoleta, as the leader of L&D, proved that learning can be both engaging and effortless.
